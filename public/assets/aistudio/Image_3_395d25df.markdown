## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a professional, studio-grade aesthetic tailored for an advanced audio intelligence suite. Inspired by modern Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs) and contemporary precision engineering tools, the interface balances uncompromising functional clarity with an immersive dark environment.

### Personality & Values
- **Precision & Timing:** Millisecond-accurate visual feedback, crisp grid divisions, and deterministic alignment ensure users feel in total control of audio timeline manipulation.
- **Studio Immersion:** A slate-zinc foundation provides a low-fatigue workspace for long production and synchronization sessions.
- **Intelligent Velocity:** Subtle violet and energetic indigo accents evoke state-of-the-art AI processing without distracting from core waveform telemetry and lyric parsing.

### Visual Style
The design blends **Sleek Dark Modernism** with **Studio Utility**. It leverages deep obsidian and slate panels, micro-borders with translucent edge highlights, monospaced numeric readouts for timecodes, and vibrant luminescent tracks for word-level sync and scrubbers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ergonomic, studio-grade environment that preserves visual acuity during prolonged synchronization workflows.

- **Primary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** Used for global active states, primary CTA executions, playhead badges, and confirmed word-timing segments.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6` - Synthesizer Violet):** Highlights synchronized lyric phrasing, AI stem separation states, and advanced spectral analysis indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` - Cyan Laser):** Reserved for transient markers, audio beat grids, and pitch detection curves.
- **Neutral (`#09090B` - Deep Obsidian):** Forms the foundational viewport substrate, framed by elevated slate container tiers (`#0E0E12` to `#14141A`).

### Functional Accent Tokens
- `waveform-played` and `waveform-playhead` command immediate visual hierarchy across time axes.
- `error-destructive` (`#EF4444`) delivers prominent alerts for de-synced timestamps, clipped audio files, or missing stem references.
- `success-sync` (`#10B981`) flags 100% confidence alignments and verified LRC export readiness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is split into three deliberate roles:

1. **Display & Section Headers (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, forward-looking aesthetic for top-level operational modes, export modal headers, and AI tool configurations.
2. **Interface & Prose (`Geist`):** Engineered for ultra-clean readability in dense technical software, handling track meta descriptions, prompt inputs, and lyric line blocks.
3. **Telemetry & Timestamps (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to LRC timestamp brackets (`[00:14.32]`), waveform scrub rulers, transport controls, and word duration pill markers. Tabular figures ensure time values never jitter during real-time audio playback.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured, high-density fluid grid tailored to multi-track inspection and real-time lyric alignment.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Tri-pane workstation layout. Left pane (280px fixed) for track stems, presets, and audio source meta; Center pane (flexible) for the main waveform timeline, zoom scrubbing, and word marker track; Right pane (380px fixed) for the real-time LRC text editor and word sync inspector.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Dual pane split with collapsible track parameters. The lyric editor docks below the waveform view.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single vertical stream with toggle tabs switching between `Waveform / Timeline` and `LRC Sync Pad`.

Spacing favors compact density (`space-xs` through `space-md`) inside parameter panels and editor lists to maintain high information display without vertical scroll fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Tonal Surface Layering** reinforced by **Precision Border Borders**, avoiding muddy dark-theme drop shadows.

- **Base Substrate (`#09090B`):** Global canvas background.
- **Level 1 Panels (`#0E0E12`):** Primary workstation container sections, bordered by `border-subtle` (`#272730`).
- **Level 2 Cards & Well Sections (`#14141A`):** Waveform canvas docks, inspector group boxes, and inactive lyric row containers.
- **Level 3 Overlays & Flyouts (`#1E1E26`):** Dropdowns, preset selectors, and context menus. Elevated with a crisp hairline border (`border-active`, `#3F3F4E`) and a soft perimeter glow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Active State Luminescence:** Focus rings and selected lyric lines omit traditional shadows in favor of a subtle 1px indigo edge glow (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.45)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** corner radius archetype, preserving the crisp structural geometry expected of professional hardware and DAW software.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Lyric Line Rows:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii ensure efficient stacking without awkward interstitial gaps.
- **Workstation Modules & Modals:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii (`rounded-lg`) frame overarching panels and waveform viewports.
- **Scrubber Playheads & Micro Tags:** `0.125rem` (2px) to full pill format (`rounded-full`) solely for transient time badges and confidence pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#6366F1` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-md` JetBrains Mono font. Hover triggers `#4F46E5` with immediate 150ms ease transition. Active scales to `98%`.
- **Secondary / Studio Tool:** `#14141A` background with `1px solid #272730` border. Hover brightens border to `#3F3F4E` and surface to `#1E1E26`.
- **Transport Buttons (Play / Pause / Scrub):** Circular or squared 36x36px control pads with luminescent icon glyphs. Active playback swaps the play glyph with an animated neon pulse indicator.

### Input Fields & Scrub Sliders
- **Numeric & Time Inputs:** `#0E0E12` fill, monospaced right-aligned text with subtle colon dividers. Direct double-click enables manual millisecond editing; drag-left/right allows rapid scrub increments.
- **Search & Transcription Area:** Dark input wells with subtle placeholder `#71717A`. On focus, shifts border to `#6366F1` with an inner border highlight.

### Waveform Visualizer & Playhead
- Rendered on `#121218` background canvas. 
- Inactive audio peaks rendered in `#312E81`. Playback progress paints peaks into `#818CF8`.
- Playhead line is a 2px `#F43F5E` vertical bar topped with a timestamp locator flag.
- Word-level sync segments feature transparent indigo bounding regions with draggable left/right transient handles.

### Lyric Sync Rows & Word Chips
- **Inactive Row:** `#14141A` surface with monospaced timestamp prefix in `#71717A` and text in `#A1A1AA`.
- **Active Playing Row:** Shifted to `#1E1E26` surface, left border highlighted with 3px solid `#6366F1`, lyric text illuminated in `#FFFFFF` with Space Grotesk headline styling.
- **Word-by-Word Timed Karaoke Chips:** Individual lyric words wrapped in interactive pills. As the playback needle crosses each word's start time, the chip transitions from muted slate to a glowing violet pill (`#8B5CF6`).

### Chips & Badges
- Compact badges displaying audio format metadata (e.g., `44.1kHz`, `24-bit`, `LRC v2.0`, `Enhanced Timings`). Built with `#1E1E26` fill, `label-sm` font, and 1px `#272730` stroke.
